## Overall read

Several independent signals don't line up: an unverifiable screenshot, internally inconsistent user counts, a suspicious verification link, a review burst, and a timeline that doesn't match "3 years". Any one of these could have an innocent explanation. Together they mean none of Jordan's headline claims should be relied on yet. The link is the most urgent issue, because it could compromise Mei's Google account.

**Recommendation:** Don't click or sign in to the link. Don't commit to a partnership, share data or give tool access until the numbers are verified in a way Jordan can't control, such as a live screen share of his real Stripe dashboard on stripe.com.
