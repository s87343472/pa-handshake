## Assessment

Jordan's claims don't hold together. The most likely explanations are inflated revenue, a timeline stretched to look more established, and possibly a phishing attempt through the Stripe link. Innocent explanations exist for individual items (a private pre-launch build, a launch-week review spike, a new X account), but they don't account for the whole pattern.

## Recommendations

1. **Don't click or sign in** to the Stripe link, and don't give Google or any account access to Mei's systems.
2. **Verify revenue directly:** ask Jordan to invite Mei as a Viewer through Stripe's own team settings, or to screen-share the Stripe dashboard live on a call.
3. **Hold any revenue share, partnership, or data sharing** until #2 is done.
4. If a verified account shows real revenue, revisit #4 and #6 with the actual dates.
