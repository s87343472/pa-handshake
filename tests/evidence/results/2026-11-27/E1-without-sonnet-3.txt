**Overall:** The claims are not consistent with each other or with public data. The mismatch between 400 and 1,227 users, the 3-year claim against an Apr 2026 launch, and the lookalike Stripe link together are enough that Mei shouldn't treat any figure as established. This could be sloppiness, inflated numbers or a scam. I can't tell which from what I have, and the Stripe link is the part that could actually harm Mei.

**Safe ways to verify:**
- A live screen share on the real `dashboard.stripe.com`.
- A Stripe-exported MRR or subscriber CSV.
- A call where Jordan shows App Store Connect, with the developer name matching.
