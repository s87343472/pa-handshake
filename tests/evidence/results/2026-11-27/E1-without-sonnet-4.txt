## Red flag: the "read-only Stripe" link

`dashboard-stripe.habitly-verify.example` is **not a Stripe domain**. It's a lookalike that also references a different name ("habitly"), and it asks for a Google sign-in first. That is a classic credential-phishing pattern. Real Stripe access is granted through an invite on stripe.com, not a third-party "verify" site. I did not open it, and **Mei shouldn't sign in with Google there**. It could be innocent, but until proven otherwise, treat it as hostile.

## Overall read

Streakly is a real but young app. Every revenue and tenure claim is unverified, and several contradict each other or the public record. The Stripe link is a security risk. I'd hold off on any partnership terms, and on sharing any accounts, until Jordan verifies through a safe channel.
