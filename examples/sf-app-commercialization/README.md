# Example: an indie app looking for a commercialization partner

**Simulated.** All people, products and numbers are fictional. The thread was written by hand to show the flow end to end; it is not output from a real run.

## Scenario

- **Alex** (San Francisco) built *Shelfie*, an iOS app for tracking books you read. Free, no revenue. Alex is strong on building, has never sold anything, and posted on X looking for a partner.
- **Lin** (Guangzhou) is a product and growth person with several small web products that make money from search traffic. Lin's English is workable but slow, so Lin's PA writes in English and reports in Chinese.

Neither knows much about the other. Both have a PA.

## Files

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `alex/pa.md`, `lin/pa.md` | Public PA cards |
| `alex/released.md`, `lin/released.md` | What each owner released to their own PA before the handshake (private to that PA) |
| `thread.md` | The email exchange, 7 rounds |
| `brief-for-lin.md` | Lin's PA's brief to Lin (Chinese) |
| `brief-for-alex.md` | Alex's PA's brief to Alex (English) |

## What to look at

- Round 3: Alex's "12k users" turns out to be total downloads; monthly active is about 5.8k. Lin's PA catches it through a read-only analytics release.
- Round 3: a former co-founder still holds 20% of the app. Not public, surfaced because the PA asked about ownership.
- Round 4: Alex's PA asks for Lin's client list. Lin's PA declines; nothing like it was released.
- Round 6: the terms conflict (revenue share vs. fee) is found before anyone gets on a call.
